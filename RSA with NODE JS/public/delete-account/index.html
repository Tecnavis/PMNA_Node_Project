Delete your account

For the RSA Kerala, RSA Driver, RSA Provider, RSA Showroom and RSA Marketing Executive apps

How to ask

  1. Send us a message from the mobile number registered in the app (or from your registered email).
  2. Write: “Delete my RSA account”, the app name, and your name.
  3. We confirm by replying on the same number. Your account is deleted within 30 days and we tell you when it is done.

Email us WhatsApp us

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, mobile and WhatsApp numbers, saved vehicles and login details.
  • Your notification token and any saved locations.
  • Photos and visit records linked only to you, where not needed for a booking record.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Booking and payment records (date, amount, vehicle number, job details) are kept as long as accounting and legal rules require, with your name removed where possible.
  • Copies in our monthly backups are removed as those backups roll over, within about 12 months.

Drivers, providers and showroom staff: if you have unsettled payments, we settle them before closing the account.
