Privacy Policy

RSA Kerala (PMNA Cranes), Perinthalmanna, Kerala, India · Last updated 7 November 2026

This policy explains what information the RSA Kerala apps collect, why, and the choices you have. It covers these Android apps: RSA Kerala (customers), RSA Driver, RSA Provider, RSA Showroom and RSA Marketing Executive, and our websites.

What we collect

AppInformation
RSA Kerala (customers)Name, mobile number, vehicle number and model, what happened to the vehicle, pickup and drop-off locations (your precise location only when you choose “use my location”), booking history and any rating you give.
RSA Driver, RSA ProviderName, mobile number, the device's precise location while you are on a job (including when the app is in the background, so customers and our control room can see the truck on the way), photos of vehicles at pickup and drop-off, payment details you record (amount, method, UPI screenshot), and a notification token so we can send you new jobs.
RSA ShowroomStaff name, mobile and WhatsApp numbers, designation, the showroom you belong to, and bookings you create (customer name, phone, vehicle number, comments).
RSA Marketing ExecutiveLogin username, showrooms you add (name, address, contact details, photos, location), and showroom visits (photo and location at the time of the visit).

We do not collect contacts, messages, call logs, or health or financial account information. We do not sell personal information and we do not show ads.

How we use it

  • To take and dispatch towing and roadside-assistance bookings, show live tracking and arrival times, and complete the job.
  • To sign you in with a one-time code (OTP) sent by SMS or WhatsApp.
  • To send booking updates, tracking links and receipts by SMS, WhatsApp or app notification.
  • To keep accounts and payment records for drivers, providers and showrooms, and to settle payments.
  • To check service quality (for example ratings, visit verification) and to prevent fraud and misuse.

Who we share it with

  • The people handling your booking: the assigned driver or provider, and the showroom involved.
  • Service providers that run parts of the service for us: cloud hosting and database, SMS (Twilio), WhatsApp messaging (Meta), app notifications (Google Firebase), and map providers. They may use the data only to provide that service.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Bookings and payment records: as long as needed for accounting and legal requirements.
  • Sign-in and activity logs: up to 1 year. Location points from live tracking: only as long as needed for the booking and its records.
  • Data backups: rolled over monthly, the oldest removed after about 12 months.

Your choices and rights

  • You can turn off location, camera or notification permissions in your phone settings; some features (live tracking, visit verification, job alerts) will not work without them.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account. See Delete your account.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Sign-in uses one-time codes and access is limited to staff who need it.

Children

The apps are not meant for children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above.
